About

Built to make the first customer interaction better.

A practical customer-response product for HVAC businesses.

Dorivien is being built around a straightforward problem: a potential customer can have a good reason to contact an HVAC company and still have a poor first experience if the response is slow, repetitive, unclear, or disconnected from the team that will actually do the work.

What Dorivien is trying to improve

The first few minutes of a customer inquiry. Dorivien is designed to respond, collect useful service information, organize the conversation, and prepare a handoff without inventing the things only the business can know or confirm.

How the product is being built

  • Business-specific: approved services, service areas, hours, policies, and escalation rules should come from the company using it.
  • Human-controlled: appointment confirmation, exceptions, unusual policies, and sensitive decisions remain with the team.
  • Safety-aware: gas, carbon monoxide, smoke, fire, and similar signals are treated differently from ordinary intake.
  • Tested before launch: normal, incomplete, urgent, unusual, and safety scenarios should be tested before a client deploys it publicly.

What Dorivien does not claim

Dorivien is currently being prepared for pilot deployments. The product is designed to be configured around a real business, tested against that business's rules, and judged on whether it improves the lead process in practice. Dorivien does not rely on invented testimonials or guaranteed performance claims.
