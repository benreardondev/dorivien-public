Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated September 5, 2026.

This policy describes the current public Dorivien website and demo. A production client deployment may require client-specific privacy terms, notices, retention rules, and agreements.

1. What this website does

Dorivien provides information about customer-response software for HVAC businesses and offers an interactive demonstration. The public demo is designed for evaluation and does not create a real service appointment.

2. Information you choose to provide

If you type information into the demo, that content is processed to generate a response and organize the example lead. You should not submit passwords, payment-card information, government identifiers, medical information, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

3. How the current demo uses information

  • To generate the customer-facing response.
  • To organize the example service request shown in the demo.
  • To protect the service from abuse and diagnose technical problems.

4. Storage and service providers

The current demo does not use an application database to persist conversation history. The active conversation is kept in browser memory while the page is open. Hosting infrastructure may process normal technical request logs. When live AI is enabled, message content is sent to the configured AI provider so a response can be generated, subject to that provider's applicable data terms and account settings.

5. Business deployments

A client deployment may connect to a CRM, booking system, messaging provider, or other business system. Those deployments should use client-approved rules for access, retention, notice, and deletion. The client remains responsible for determining what information it is permitted to collect and how it must be handled.

6. Cookies and analytics

The current public build does not include third-party advertising trackers or analytics scripts. If analytics are added later, this policy should be updated before they are enabled.

7. Contact

Until a dedicated privacy email is created, privacy questions can be raised through the contact or booking path on the main Dorivien website.
