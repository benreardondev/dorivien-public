FAQ

Questions before a walkthrough.

Does Dorivien replace our office staff?+

No. It is designed to handle repetitive first-response and intake work, then provide a cleaner handoff to the people already running the business.

Can it book appointments automatically?+

Only when a real booking workflow is connected and the business explicitly wants that behavior. Dorivien should never invent availability or claim an appointment is confirmed when it is not.

Can it answer questions about our business?+

Yes, once the deployment is configured with approved business information. If Dorivien does not have an approved answer, the safer behavior is to say the team can confirm it rather than guess.

What happens in a safety situation?+

Potential gas, carbon monoxide, smoke, fire, and similar immediate safety concerns are handled differently from ordinary intake and trigger safety-first guidance.

What do you need from us to set it up?+

Business hours, service area, services, intake questions, escalation rules, handoff preferences, and the workflow you want customers to follow.

Is the public demo a finished client deployment?+

No. It demonstrates the conversation and intake flow. A client deployment still needs the company’s approved information, rules, integrations, testing, and sign-off.
